title: Change personal details
description: Getting help with changes to your name, address or contact details.
---

To change your address, name or contact details, contact reception on [01625&nbsp;529421](tel:+441625529421) or ask at the surgery.

The practice's existing website says address changes can be made through Accurx or by collecting a form at the practice. It says name changes require a visit to the practice for identity checks. Ask reception what you need to bring for your request.

Do not send personal information through this website. It has no change-of-details form.
