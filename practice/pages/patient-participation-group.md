The Patient Participation Group (PPG) was established in 2006 to provide a link between patients and the healthcare professionals at Handforth Health Centre.

The group represents patients' interests, helps communication between patients and staff, discusses issues and suggests improvements.

The practice website says the group meets 3 to 4 times a year and welcomes new members. Ask reception on [01625&nbsp;529421](tel:+441625529421) about joining or getting copies of meeting minutes.

This website does not have a PPG sign-up form.
