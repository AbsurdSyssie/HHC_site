The practice's existing self-referral page lists the services below. These links take you to external organisations. Check the service's own information about access, eligibility and referrals.

- [Physiotherapy](https://physio.easterncheshireccg.nhs.uk/)
- [NHS East Cheshire Talking Therapies](https://www.thebiglifegroup.com/service/talking-therapies-eastern-cheshire/)
- [One You Cheshire East](https://oneyoucheshireeast.org/)
- [East Cheshire NHS Trust podiatry](https://services.eastcheshire.nhs.uk/podiatry/self-referral-form)
- [East Cheshire NHS Trust antenatal care](https://services.eastcheshire.nhs.uk/maternity/self-referral-form-antenatal-care)

The practice says it cannot give updates on a referral once it has been sent to these services. Contact the service about your referral.

If you cannot use an online service, contact reception on [01625&nbsp;529421](tel:+441625529421) for help finding a contact route.
