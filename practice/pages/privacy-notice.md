For the full practice privacy notice, contact Handforth Health Centre on [01625&nbsp;529421](tel:+441625529421) or ask at reception.

The existing practice website has a [Your Data page](https://www.handforthhealthcentre.nhs.uk/policies/your-data/) with privacy notices and information about medical records. Its documents need to be checked by the practice's Data Protection Officer before they are reproduced here.

This is a holding page, not a replacement for the practice's full privacy notice.

## Medical records and data choices

Ask reception for information about access to your medical records, correcting records and data-sharing choices. Do not send personal or medical information through this website. It has no patient forms.

[NHS information about your data choices](https://www.nhs.uk/your-nhs-data-matters/).
