## Freedom of information

The practice's published codes of practice say information about administering public funds can be requested in writing from the practice manager.

## Respect for patients and staff

The practice's published policy asks patients to treat staff courteously and says violent or abusive behaviour is not tolerated. Ask reception for the full current policy.
