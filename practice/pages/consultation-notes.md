title: Consultation notes and your choice
description: The practice's information about AI-assisted consultation notes.

The practice's existing website describes using an AI medical scribe to help clinicians prepare consultation notes. It says clinicians review and approve the notes before adding them to your record.

The practice says use of the scribe is optional. If you prefer not to use it, tell reception when booking or tell your clinician at the start of the consultation. The website says your care will not be affected and your clinician will take notes manually instead.

Ask the practice for its patient information and data privacy FAQ. Phone [01625&nbsp;529421](tel:+441625529421) or ask at reception.

This website does not record consultations or use an AI scribe.
