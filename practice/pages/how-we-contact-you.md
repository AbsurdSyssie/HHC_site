The practice's published guidance, observed on 29 October 2026, says email is its main way to contact patients from April 2026. It asks patients to check their inbox after sending a routine or administrative request through online triage. The guidance says the practice may phone if it needs to contact someone urgently. These details have not been confirmed by the practice.

The practice's email-update form link was broken when checked, so this page does not provide an email update link. Please phone the practice to check or change your contact details.
