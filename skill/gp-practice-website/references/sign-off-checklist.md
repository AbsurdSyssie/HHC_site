# Sign-off checklist

Go through every item with the person before publishing. Ask them to answer yes to each one. If the answer is no, fix it and check again.

## Getting help

1. The urgent help box at the bottom of every page says to call 999 in an emergency and 111 when the practice is closed.
2. The main phone number is correct. (Ask them to compare it with their phone system or letterhead.)
3. The phone line hours are correct.
4. The online form link opens the practice's real online consultation form. (Send them the link and ask them to open it.)
5. Opening times are correct for every day, including any branch surgeries.
6. Any planned closures are listed with the right dates.

## Practice information

7. All addresses and postcodes are correct.
8. Everyone named on the team page is happy to be listed, and their roles are right.
9. Services listed are ones the practice actually offers.
10. The registration area is right.

## Required information

11. The complaints contact is correct.
12. The privacy notice is the practice's own (or a holding page, with the full version requested from the Data Protection Officer).
13. England only: the CQC link goes to the practice's own CQC page.
14. England only: the GP earnings figures match the practice's declaration.

## Final checks

15. The "Demo" banner is gone.
16. The person who signed off is happy to be recorded as having checked the site today. Use this date for `site.content_last_reviewed`.

Record who signed off and when in your reply to the person, so they have a note of it for the practice's records.
